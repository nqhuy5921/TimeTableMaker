## 4.0.0 — 2026-11-02

Release 4: profiles, exporting to Excel, and teachers' phone numbers. Covers PF-1 to PF-3, IE-2, DM-1's phone, and TC-4's question before exporting with errors. Ships without the PDF export (IE-4), which the user set aside on 2026-10-29. Signed off by the user on 2026-11-02.

## 3.0.0 — 2026-10-28

Release 3: the Classes, Rooms, and Subjects pages. Covers DM-2 to DM-4. Signed off by the user on 2026-10-28.

- A Classes page lists every class in class-bar order with its homeroom teacher, assistant homeroom teacher, periods placed out of 50, and current errors. Classes can be added, edited (a class renamed into another grade keeps its grid and moves in the class bar), and deleted, which takes its placements off the grid and leaves its cards in the pool.

- A Subjects page lists every subject as a tag in its colors, with its cards. Subjects can be added and renamed, and their colors picked: from the name, one of the 15 palettes, or custom background and text. Deleting a subject takes its cards, their placements, and any teacher subject limits naming it.

- The workbook's form teacher and co-teacher are called the homeroom teacher and the assistant homeroom teacher everywhere on screen.
- Saved files move to format 4 (a subject's chosen colors); older ones open unchanged.
- Not yet tested on Windows: as with releases 1 and 2, the Windows install test waits until the whole app is complete (F-1). Still to come: teachers' phone numbers arrive with release 4's export, and weekly totals (DM-6) were dropped.
